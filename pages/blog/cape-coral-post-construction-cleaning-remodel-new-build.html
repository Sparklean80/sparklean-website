Post-Construction Cleaning in Cape Coral, FL: Remodel & New Build — What You Need Before Move-In

Sparklean Cleaning · Cape Coral, Fort Myers, Naples, Bonita Springs & Estero

If you are searching for post-construction cleaning in Cape Coral, FL, you are likely near closing on a remodel or new build — and still seeing fine dust on cabinets, in HVAC returns, and along lanai door tracks. Standard housekeeping cannot reset a jobsite. Sparklean’s post-construction program is built for exactly that.

We serve Cape Harbour, Tarpon Point, Sandoval, Cape Royal, and all Cape Coral zip codes. Map scope on our Cape Coral cleaning service page, then share your punch-list or closing date.

Remodel vs. New Build — Same Dust Problem

Kitchen and bath remodels leave drywall compound, grout haze, and trade footprints in lived-in homes. New construction in Cape Coral often means lanai expansions, impact windows, and canal-front builds with dust in every closet and cabinet box. Both need a builder’s clean — not a weekly maid visit.

Post-Construction vs. Regular House Cleaning

  • Multi-pass dusting of walls, trim, doors, and ledges
  • Interior windows, tracks, and sticker residue
  • Cabinet and closet interiors and exteriors
  • Careful treatment of new stone, tile, and fresh paint
  • HEPA-focused vacuuming and damp mopping for silica and drywall dust

Rough Clean vs. Final Clean

Many Cape Coral projects need two phases: a rough clean after major trades exit, and a final clean after last touch-ups — before owner walkthrough or move-in. We coordinate with your GC or designer timeline.

Who Calls Sparklean in Cape Coral

  • Homeowners closing on a new canal-front build
  • Investors preparing a flip or rental in Sandoval or Coral Lakes
  • Property managers after tenant improvement work
  • Builders needing punch-list-ready detail before handoff
